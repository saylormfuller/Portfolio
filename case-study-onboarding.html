Helping 130,000+ Brand Affiliates Build Momentum Toward Their First Earnings

Designed an onboarding experience within the Stela app to help new affiliates take their first steps toward earning income.

Company

Nu Skin

Date

Jul 2023 - Aug 2023

Team

Product Owners, Nu Skin Brand Affiliates, Customer Experience

My Role

UX/UI Designer and UX Researcher

Overview

Nu Skin has traditionally relied on Brand Affiliates to onboard and train new members, leading to inconsistent guidance and lower early engagement.

During a company-wide UX Hackathon, I collaborated with Product, Customer Experience, and UX teams to design an onboarding experience within the Stela app that helps new affiliates take their first steps toward earning income.

By providing structured resources and actionable guidance, the feature supports users in reaching key early milestones and building long-term success.

Problem

Nu Skin's onboarding experience depended almost entirely on peer-led training from existing Brand Affiliates. While some new affiliates received excellent mentorship, many were left to navigate the business and the Stela platform on their own, resulting in inconsistent onboarding experiences and lower early engagement.

Internal data revealed a critical business insight: Brand Affiliates who earned their first $100 within six months were significantly more likely to remain active long-term. Yet without structured guidance, many new affiliates never completed the key actions, such as creating their first Product Offer, that helped them reach this milestone.

The opportunity was to create a scalable onboarding experience that could provide every new affiliate with the knowledge and confidence needed to succeed, regardless of whether they had an engaged mentor.

Solution

To reduce reliance on peer-led training, we designed an in-app onboarding experience within Stela that provides new Brand Affiliates with structured guidance from their very first day.

Rather than overwhelming users with information, the experience introduces the highest-impact business activities through clear, actionable steps that help affiliates create their first Product Offer, invite customers, and begin earning income with confidence.

By embedding onboarding directly into the product, the experience creates a consistent learning journey that supports users in reaching the milestones most closely tied to long-term engagement and retention.

Process

Understanding Why New Affiliates Lose Momentum

To understand why many new affiliates failed to reach critical early milestones, I mapped the onboarding journey from account creation through their first earnings.

The visualization revealed a clear pattern. Affiliates who received guidance from an engaged upline learned the platform, created their first Product Offer, and were more likely to earn $100 within their first six months—a milestone Nu Skin identified as a strong predictor of long-term retention. Those without structured support often struggled to learn independently, never reached that earnings benchmark, and were significantly less likely to continue selling beyond their first year.

This exercise reframed the challenge from simply teaching users how to use Stela to designing a scalable onboarding system that helps every new affiliate reach the behaviors and earnings milestones most closely tied to long-term success.

Understanding Early Success

As part of a company-wide UX Hackathon, I collaborated with Product Owners, Customer Experience, and fellow designers to explore solutions for improving new affiliate onboarding. Through collaborative brainstorming and affinity mapping, we generated and prioritized ideas before selecting a concept to prototype and validate.

Turning Insights Into Action

Working alongside another UX Designer, I translated our ideas into a high-fidelity prototype within a single day. Our goal was to create an onboarding experience that was simple, actionable, and motivating, while incorporating small moments of delight to keep new users engaged.

Learning From Real Affiliates

We tested the prototype in the Nu Skin headquarters lobby with Brand Affiliates attending a company event. Participants responded positively to the concept and immediately recognized the value of having guided onboarding within Stela.

Key Insights

  • Users were excited to see a dedicated onboarding experience.
  • Affiliates believed the feature would make training new recruits significantly easier.
  • Many wanted the ability to revisit onboarding content after completing it.

Validating With New Users

To validate the experience with first-time users, we conducted a moderated usability study through UserZoom. Participants completed onboarding tasks while thinking aloud and shared feedback on clarity and navigation.

Additional Insights

Users found the onboarding flow intuitive and easy to navigate. Participants wanted additional context and coaching throughout the experience.

“A little more explanation on each page could be helpful. I was confused on the discount step, but a simple explanation would have made it make sense.” — New Brand Affiliate

Evolving the Experience

Outcome

Nu Skin's internal data identified a critical predictor of long-term success: Brand Affiliates who earn their first $100 within six months are significantly more likely to remain active beyond their first year. However, many new affiliates never reached this milestone because onboarding relied heavily on inconsistent peer-led training rather than structured, in-product guidance.

Our research showed that affiliates needed ongoing coaching-not a one-time onboarding experience. Based on these insights, we recommended a progressive onboarding system that guides users toward the next most impactful action, including creating their first Product Offer, inviting customers to Vera, and sharing sign-up invites.

Designed for 130,000+ Brand Affiliates using the Stela app, this approach creates a scalable onboarding experience that reduces cognitive load, builds confidence through incremental wins, and helps more users progress toward the first $100 milestone most closely associated with long-term retention.

What's Next

When this concept was designed, AI-powered product experiences were still in their infancy. The onboarding journey provided a structured path that guided every new affiliate through the same foundational milestones.

Today, that experience could evolve into an intelligent coaching system. By analyzing each Brand Affiliate's progress, such as whether they've created a Product Offer, invited customers to Vera, or completed other key business activities, AI could identify gaps in their journey and deliver personalized recommendations at the moment they're needed most.

Rather than presenting the same onboarding to every user, the experience could adapt in real time by providing contextual coaching, interactive walkthroughs, educational resources, and next-step recommendations based on each affiliate's goals and progress.

This evolution would transform onboarding from a one-time experience into an ongoing business coach, helping more than 130,000 Brand Affiliates build confidence, achieve key performance milestones, and strengthen long-term retention.
